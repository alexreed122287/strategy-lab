What the name means

Relentless Outperformers - the gate: RS252 > +40% vs SPY at the signal. Only names that beat the market for the past year qualify; the ungated version is nearly a coin flip through the cycle (OOS PF 1.157). Briefly Embarrassed - the trigger: RSI(2) < 10 above the 200-day SMA. A two-day oversold print in a leader is a lapse of dignity, not a breakdown. Rapidly Traded - in at the next open, out at the close over the SMA5; median hold 2.6 bars.

On the fills, call him Bob - all he does is bounce.

Reading the comparison - same signal, different vehicle, different throughput

Win rates are nearly identical in both vehicles (68-73%) - the vehicle doesn't change whether trades win. The calls pay 5-7x per trade (single names move more than indexes on a bounce, and DITM calls carry more effective leverage than a 3x fund) and fire 2.5-3x as often (77 gated names vs four correlated index sources whose signals mostly overlap and collapse to one position). The live shares config keeps ~95% of capital in cash with −6 to −10% drawdowns and zero execution uncertainty; ROBERT compounds 59-108% at −22 to −30% drawdowns with option friction still to be proven live. Shares config sim: SMH→SOXL, SPY→UPRO, QQQ→TQQQ, XLK→TECL; 2 slots × 27.5%; one index ETF at a time; SOXL never alongside TQQQ/TECL.

The go-live gate

The one unmeasured variable is realized option friction. The backtest models 1.25% of premium on entries and 2.5% on exits; the edge breaks even near 4x those numbers. Twenty logged fills decide it - the Paper-Fill Log is the instrument. Until that gate passes, ROBERT stays research: no allocation, no x-number.
