What these numbers mean

  • Rank score (0-100) = the average of three percentiles - profit factor, win rate, and avg return per trade - measured against every other arm in your current filter and window. 90 means top decile on the blend. It re-ranks live when you change the Rank window.
  • Arm CAGR is NOT the stock's return. It is what compounding ONLY this ticker's signals under this strategy would have earned - in the trade when signaled, in cash otherwise (low exposure = lots of cash time). The Stock B&H column beside it IS the stock: buy-and-hold CAGR over the same window, so you can see whether the strategy actually beat just owning the name.
  • Rank window re-scores arms on only the trades exited inside that window (Full / last 3y / last 1y). The VETTED badge always reflects the full-window bar (n>=20, PF>=1.3, win>=60, profitable in both eras; an arm with no closed trades in one era cannot be vetted, however strong the other era looks) - short windows re-rank but never re-vet, so a hot year can't promote an unproven arm.

Survivor-biased grade-B tickers are excluded from this dashboard entirely. LEV rows use measured-drag synthetic vehicles.
