Method - what this page measures and why mid is the reference

Slip vs mid is the realized cost of one side: for a buy, (fill − mid) / mid; for a sell, (mid − fill) / mid. Positive = you paid; negative = you filled inside the mid. The mid is the reference because the quoted spread overstates realistic cost 2-3x on DITM calls (measured on real closing NBBO, 08/06/2026) - judging fills by the screen width would fail names that execute fine.

Model is what the locked backtest assumed: 1.25% of premium on entries (09:45 limit at mid), 2.5% on exits (close, limit worked from mid). Multiple = your realized slip ÷ the modeled number for that side, floored at 0 when you beat the mid.

The gate: the production edge (+7-9% per trade through the cycle) breaks even when friction reaches roughly 4x modeled. Blended multiple ≤1.5x means the backtest numbers are conservative; 1.5-2.5x means live results will run below backtest but positive; above 3.5x, do not go live. Twenty fills (both sides represented) is the sample floor - the same n-discipline as x38.

Where fills live. The durable store is data/paper_fills.json in the repo, written only by scripts/log_fill.py - so every fill is versioned, attributable to a commit, and readable by the analysis scripts directly. Until 08/26/2026 the log lived in one browser's localStorage, where clearing site data destroyed it silently: a go-live gate a cache eviction can reset is not a gate. Entry still happens in the browser, because bid and ask have to be captured at the moment the order is worked, but a browser-only row is a draft - it does not count toward the twenty, and the panel above it says so until it is committed. The store is public, like the rest of this site; keep account identifiers out of the notes field.

Entry discipline while logging: limit at mid or better, ~09:45 ET; skip anything that will not fill inside the spread. A missed trade costs nothing; a bad fill is the one variable this page exists to measure honestly - do not chase to complete the log.
